Project Events

September

Democracy Week Workshops

During the University of Michigan’s Democracy Week, Walk Cycle to the Polls held two free, hands-on workshops — part art class, part civic celebration, linking the history of marching for voting rights to the simple joy of making something move. Participants posed their own hand-drawn characters in 1-, 4-, or 8-frame walk cycles, on their own devices or on paper templates that were scanned on the spot and turned into playable characters for the video game. No experience was required, and voter registration resources were on hand.

James Baxter Visits Walk Cycle to the Polls

Legendary animator James Baxter (Disney, DreamWorks, Warner Bros.) came to the University of Michigan for three public events.

  • Wednesday, October 28 · 1–3 p.m.

    Walk Cycle to the Polls Tournament

    Duderstadt Center Atrium

    Our early voting tournament! Players raced characters from the Walk Cycle to the Polls video game to the virtual polls, and James Baxter signed autographs. Want your own character in the next race? Learn how to submit one.

    Photos coming soon.

  • Thursday, October 29 · 10:30 a.m.–1 p.m.

    Animation Demo with James Baxter

    Art & Architecture Auditorium, Room 2104

    Baxter animated an 8-frame walk cycle for the Walk Cycle to the Polls video game, plus a quadruped walk cycle.

  • Thursday, October 29 · 5:30–7 p.m.

    “Animation, It’s Alive” — Penny Stamps Speaker Series

    Michigan Theatre

    James Baxter gave the Penny Stamps Distinguished Speaker Series talk.
