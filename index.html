The Project

Animation. History. Democracy.

Walk Cycle to the Polls transforms the act of voting into a joyful, creative public event. Participants learn to animate their own 8-frame walk cycle characters, then race those characters to a virtual polling booth — before casting their real-world ballots.

Animate

Create Your Character

Learn the fundamentals of walk cycle animation from the tradition of Richard Williams and other masters. Draw 8 frames and bring your character to life.

Race

Enter the Tournament

Submit your character to the Walk Cycle to the Polls video game and race against other community members at early voting sites and public events.

Vote

Cast Your Ballot

After racing your avatar to the virtual polls, head to an early voting location and make your voice heard. Art and civic action, together.

Fall 2026 — The Semiquincentennial Update

Bigger. Louder. More Accessible.

For the 2026 midterm elections, the project expands from a single classroom into a university-wide and community-facing initiative — with public workshops, a visit from legendary animator James Baxter, and tournaments at early voting sites across Ann Arbor.
