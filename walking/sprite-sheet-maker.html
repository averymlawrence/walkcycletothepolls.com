Sprite Sheet Maker

Turn an 8-frame GIF or 8 PNGs into a sprite sheet for the Walk Cycle to the Polls game.

Make your sprite sheet

Your files need:

  • Either one GIF with 8 frames, or 8 PNGs (one per frame)
  • Every frame exactly 800 × 800 px
  • A transparent background

PNGs are put in order by file name (e.g. walk_01.png … walk_08.png). Your files never leave your computer — the sprite sheet is made right here in your browser.

Your sprite sheet is ready
